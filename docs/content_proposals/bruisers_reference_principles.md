# Bruisers Reference Principles

## Reference Boundary

`Bruisers 2D Boxing` is an experience reference for loop density, combat readability, and accumulated consequences. MAWS must not copy its code, audio, text, characters, art, UI layout, or packaged assets.

Primary public reference: <https://store.steampowered.com/app/3740090/Bruisers_2D_Boxing/>

## What MAWS Should Learn

1. **The fight validates the schedule.** Training, recovery, work, and preparation should change what the player can safely attempt in the next fight.
2. **Distance must be readable.** A player should understand why guard, retreat, pressure, or a straight-line attack fits the current exchange without reading a formula.
3. **Consequences should compound.** Fatigue, injury, missed opportunities, relationships, and public heat should survive the result screen.
4. **A career needs competing needs.** Time spent training is time not spent earning, recovering, investigating, or maintaining relationships.
5. **Alternative routes need costs.** Media, evidence, negotiation, and withdrawal can solve situations, but change authenticity, heat, trust, or later rules.
6. **Retro visuals need modern feedback.** Pixel art is not permission for unclear silhouettes, weak hit feedback, tiny controls, or flat result screens.

## MAWS-Specific Translation

| Reference principle | MAWS implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Preparation changes the fight | Time dosage, fatigue, learned skills, equipped recipes |
| Timing and distance matter | Enemy tell, target choice, 1-2 action queue |
| Styles create different answers | Wild pressure, guard counter, cool exit, pull and tag, boxing one-two |
| Damage accumulates | Existing fatigue/injury fields; no new medical economy in Day 1-9 |
| Career choices branch | NPC memory, event follow-ups, media/evidence/rules routes |
| Fight result tells a story | Objective tiers, structured reward deltas, readable combat logs |

## Day 1-9 Application

- Day 1-3: teach that small city choices consume time and are remembered.
- Day 5: use E01 as a reliable distance test, not an early punching bag.
- Day 8: measure reading, defense, and one effective tactical recipe; do not require KO.
- Day 9: convert the measured weakness into a visible jab/straight training route.
- Each fight should leave one immediate feedback line, one persistent consequence, and one clear next action.

## Explicit Non-Goals

- Do not turn MAWS into a direct-control boxing game.
- Do not add a full aging, weight-cut, gambling, doping, or professional ranking simulation to the Day 1-9 slice.
- Do not replace MAWS's Chinese city setting, NPC relationships, traditional-martial-arts theme, or non-combat resolutions.
